Reconciliation rules

Grouping rule: Cafe remains separate; all non-Cafe vendor rows for the same SKU/date are combined. Indent Total uses the maximum repeated Total; Packets Made and Allocation are summed across combined rows. Current scope: Closing Stock and GRN are not included yet. The allocation sheet mapping is Date, SKU ID, SKU name, Total (Indent), Allocation (Allocation and assumed Dispatch), Packets made. Store-wise STN rows show destination_id as the store code; actual store names require a store master mapping. SKU filter supports typing SKU ID or SKU name. Allocation = dispatch is your stated assumption, not independent proof of physical dispatch. The Unfulfilled Orders tab lists destination-store STNs where received_qty is lower than ordered_qty, with short quantity = ordered_qty − received_qty. If the allocation sheet's Total/Allocation columns are not in comparable units to STN quantities, interpret differences carefully.
